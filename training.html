How a programme runs

Every session is hands-on and built around the problems your team actually has.

  1. UnderstandA short call to learn your team's level, tools and the reports or processes that cause the most pain.
  2. TailorI pick the right modules and build exercises on realistic, domain-relevant data.
  3. BuildLive, hands-on sessions. Participants build alongside me rather than watching slides.
  4. ApplyEach participant finishes with a working report or flow they can take straight back to their job.
